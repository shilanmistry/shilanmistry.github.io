Privacy policy

Sutra. Last updated 30 October 2026.

Sutra is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, Shilan Mistry, who signs in with his own Google account. This policy says what Sutra does with the Google data it can reach.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, Sutra reads the events in his Google Calendars, and creates or changes events and sends invitations only when he asks it to.

How the data is used

Calendar data is used only to show the owner his own schedule on his own computers (for example, a list of the week ahead and reminders to leave for appointments) and to create the events he asks for.

Where it is kept

Sutra runs on the owner's own Macs, and the calendar data it fetches is kept there, in his private files. Sutra has no server of its own.

Sharing

Google data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone for their own purposes. The owner works with an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) on his computers; when he asks it about his schedule, the calendar entries it reads pass through that service to answer him. Otherwise Google data goes to no one except as needed to carry out what the owner asks (such as Google sending an invitation he created).

Google API Services User Data Policy

Sutra's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Sutra's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete its local files from his computers.
