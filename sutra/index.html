Sutra

A personal calendar tool, used by one person on his own computers.

Sutra is a private set of scripts that Shilan Mistry runs on his own Macs to organise his work. Its Google sign-in lets it read his own Google Calendar and, when he asks, add events and send invitations from it.

It is not a product or a service. It has no other users, is not offered to the public and cannot be signed up for.

How it handles Google data is set out in the privacy policy.
